Graph neural networks (GNNs) Graph Neural Network (GNN) , which are capable of learning representations from graphical data, are naturally suitable for modeling molecular systems. This review introduces GNNs Graph Neural Network (GNN) and their various applications for small organic molecules. GNNs Graph Neural Network (GNN) rely on message-passing operations, a generic yet powerful framework, to update node features iteratively. Many researches design GNN Graph Neural Network (GNN) architectures to effectively learn topological information of 2D molecule graphs as well as geometric information of 3D molecular systems. GNNs Graph Neural Network (GNN) have been implemented in a wide variety of molecular applications, including molecular property prediction Molecular property prediction , molecular scoring and docking, molecular optimization Molecular optimization and de novo generation De novo molecular generation , molecular dynamics Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulation, etc. Besides, the review also summarizes the recent development of self-supervised learning Self-supervised learning for molecules with GNNs Graph Neural Network (GNN) .
