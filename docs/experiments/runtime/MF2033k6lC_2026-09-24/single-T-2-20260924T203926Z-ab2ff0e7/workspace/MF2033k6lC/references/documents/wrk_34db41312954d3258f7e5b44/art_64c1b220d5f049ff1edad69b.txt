AI-based song generation is rapidly advancing, using deep learning to create new and creative music. AI–generated music can create personalized soundtracks for different users and help artists come up with new musical ideas. In recent times, Large Language Models(LLMs) and transformer–based frameworks, music generated by these models sounds more natural, well–structured, and more flexible than before. This paper reviews all of the latest AI models used in music generation, including transformer models such as MusicGen and hybrid approaches that combine different neural network types . This study compares the datasets and tools used in research, including the MusicGen pre–training data set, the Million Piano-roll Dataset and other data sets built for specific styles or genres. Observations show that transformers are great for making well-structured music, while GANs are better at adding creativity and variation. LLMs are the most powerful and adaptable, but the field still needs better evaluation methods and more diverse data. To create AI music generation better, work should focus on common evaluation standards, global music data and smartly combined AI models.
