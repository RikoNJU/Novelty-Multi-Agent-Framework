State-of-health (SOH) assessment is essential for ensuring the reliability and safety of lithium-ion batteries in electric vehicles. Traditional methods relying on time-series signals or handcrafted features often fail to capture complex degradation patterns across heterogeneous data modalities. We propose a multimodal SOH assessment framework that integrates electrochemical signals with pseudo-image representations generated by a generative adversarial network (GAN). An attention-guided fusion mechanism adaptively combines features from a CNN branch for images and an LSTM branch for temporal signals, enabling the model to focus on the most informative patterns. Experiments on multiple benchmark battery datasets show that our approach consistently surpasses unimodal and early-fusion baselines. On the XJTU battery dataset, it reduces the RMSE from 0.015722 to 0.006833 and improves R² from 0.888068 to 0.978852. This framework not only delivers accurate SOH estimation but also provides interpretable fusion of physical and visual indicators, offering a robust and generalizable solution for real-world battery prognostics.
