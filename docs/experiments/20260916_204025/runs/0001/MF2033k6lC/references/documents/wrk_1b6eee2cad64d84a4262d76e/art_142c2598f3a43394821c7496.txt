Spatial-temporal sequence forecasting (STSF) is a long-standing research problem with widespread real-world applications. The neural architecture search (NAS), which automates neural network design, is essential to adaptively model the correlated dependencies of spatial and temporal features for diverse downstream tasks in STSF. However, existing methods overlook the complex interaction between spatial and temporal operators, which causes a large number of irregular local optima obstructing both heuristic and gradient-based NAS methods. Despite the emerging LLM-based methods improve it with inner knowledge, there still lacks enough mechanism for memory and task decomposed instruction. In this paper, we propose a multilevel enhanced LLM-based NAS method for STSF. Instead of directly question-querying with LLM, we decompose the STSF NAS task into three levels, and inspire the LLM’s capability with an interactive multilevel enhancement mechanism. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves competitive effectiveness against 8 baselines in 5 datasets for STSF.
