Accurate forecasting of electric vehicle (EV) charging demand is essential for maintaining power-grid stability and supporting the efficient operation of urban charging infrastructure. However, EV charging demand exhibits strong spatio-temporal heterogeneity, multi-scale fluctuations, and complex dependencies, making accurate forecasting challenging. To address these issues, this paper proposes a physics-informed spatio-temporal graph learning framework for EV charging demand forecasting. Specifically, PSO-optimized MVMD is used to reconstruct multi-regional occupancy sequences, while a 2D-CNN is employed to capture temporal interaction patterns between occupancy and electricity price. A GAT-GFC-BiGRU-TPA architecture is then developed to model spatial dependencies and key temporal dynamics across charging regions. In addition, a physics-informed meta-learning strategy with a price-sensitivity regularization term is introduced to better capture the demand-response relationship between charging demand and electricity price while improving cross-region adaptability and training stability. Experiments on a real-world EV charging dataset show that the proposed framework consistently outperforms competitive baselines across multiple forecasting horizons. Ablation studies further verify the effectiveness of the major components. Overall, the proposed framework effectively captures complex spatio-temporal dynamics and improves short- and medium-term EV charging demand forecasting.
