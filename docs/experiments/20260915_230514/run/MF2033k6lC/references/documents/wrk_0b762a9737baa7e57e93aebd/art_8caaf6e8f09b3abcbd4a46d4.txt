Dynamic Graph Neural Networks (DGNNs) commonly utilize attention mechanisms to capture spatio-temporal dependencies. However, these methods often derive attention weights based on feature similarity, leading to an insufficient utilization of explicit topological evolution signals. This limitation hinders the model’s ability to perceive distinct structural roles within evolving graphs. To address this issue, we introduce Knowledge-Enhanced Spatio-Temporal ModuleKESTM (KES TM), a unified framework that bridges lightweight structural priors with adaptive spatio-temporal attention. KESTM comprises three key components: (1) a Knowledge Enhancement Module (KEM) that encodes multi-scale degree statistics and structural embeddings into learnable node-level priors; (2) an Adaptive Spatio-Temporal Encoder (ASTE) that employs a dual-stream attention mechanism with sparsity-aware gating to dynamically balance structural and temporal signals; and (3) a Dynamic Temporal-Contrastive Loss (DTCL) that jointly handles class imbalance and temporal inconsistency. Extensive experiments on four real-world dynamic graph benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework. In particular, KESTM shows significant robustness under extreme graph sparsity, effectively addressing the Sparsity-Performance Paradox. Code is available at https://github.com/tyut2018/KESTM .
