Accurate traffic forecasting requires effective modeling of complex spatio-temporal dependencies and reliable uncertainty estimation. Although existing graph-based methods have achieved strong performance, most focus on deterministic prediction and do not explicitly incorporate structural information into probabilistic modeling. In this paper, we propose HBPC-GWN, a hierarchical Bayesian calibration framework for graph-based traffic forecasting. Rather than imposing structural constraints during training, the proposed method performs posterior-level calibration by integrating a Laplacian-based prior into the predictive distribution. This enables a principled combination of data-driven predictions and graph-structured regularization, improving the consistency between spatial structure and uncertainty estimation. We instantiate the framework on Graph WaveNet and evaluate it on multiple real-world traffic datasets. Experimental results show that HBPC-GWN achieves competitive and more stable performance across forecasting horizons. In particular, it provides consistent improvements in medium- and long-term forecasting and improves probabilistic calibration, as reflected by better negative log-likelihood and prediction interval coverage. These results demonstrate that incorporating structural priors at the posterior level is an effective and flexible strategy for enhancing both predictive accuracy and uncertainty estimation in traffic forecasting.
