Spatio-temporal traffic forecasting still suffers from three linked limitations: balanced graph operators mismatch open road networks, sequence-style SSMs ignore real graph topology, and physics losses may become self-referential. We propose UOT-GraphSSM, which combines an unbalanced optimal transport (UOT) layer, a graph-native anisotropic SSM recurrence, and a stop-gradient CTM physics loss. The UOT layer produces a mass-relaxed asymmetric transport plan for directional boundary flux; the GraphSSM recurrence propagates hidden states along this plan rather than along a serialized node order; and the CTM loss provides non-circular physical regularisation. On PEMS04/07/08, UOT-GraphSSM achieves consistent state-of-the-art results, reducing MSE by 7–25%, lowering long-horizon degradation from 65% to 43%, and retaining a compact 1.6 M-parameter footprint.
