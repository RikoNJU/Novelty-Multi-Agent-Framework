The advancement of intelligent transportation systems (ITS) has necessitated the effective integration of diverse, multi-source traffic data. Conventional approaches reliant on singular structured data struggle to encapsulate the complementarity of diverse modal data, and therefore exhibit limits in intricate traffic environments. In recent years, the cross-modal fusion-based multi-modal knowledge representation learning (KRL) method has significantly improved the semantic depth and resilience of traffic entity representation by amalgamating text, images, time series, and structured graph data. This paper examines the current state of cross-modal data fusion in intelligent transportation, grounded in the theory of multi-source heterogeneous information and knowledge representation learning. It identifies existing technical challenges and explores future avenues for innovation, with the objective of establishing a novel approach to intelligent transportation control driven by KRL. Keywords: intelligent transportation management; knowledge representation learning; multi-source heterogeneous data; cross-modal data integration; massive language model.
