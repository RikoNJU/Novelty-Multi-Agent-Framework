Audio DeepFake detection has advanced significantly in recent years mainly due to competitions like ASVSpoof and ADD; however, these competitions have been focused only on English and Chinese languages; this situation has impacted the field, having fewer experiments performed on other languages. In this paper a focus on detection models to measure their performance across different Spanish datasets, the use of RawBoost data augmentation technique and pre-training on English dataset were explored. A comparison between two popular detection models is presented, mainly the Audio Anti-Spoofing using the Integrated Spectro-Temporal (AASIST) network and Mel-Spectrogram with Residual Network (Spec-ResNet). An evaluation has been conducted on four Spanish deepfake datasets HABLA, HISPASpoof, ODSS and MLAAD in order to assess the cross-dataset generalization capabilities of the models, as well as utilizing the ASVSpoof2019 dataset as a pre-training step is also explored. Results show that the use of RawBoost data augmentation can improve detection metrics, however improvements are minimal and inconsistent across datasets and models, additionally, the pre-trained models on English dataset consistently enhanced metrics on Spanish datasets for AASIST model, and worsen them for the Spec-ResNet model.
