Spatio-temporal graph neural networks on directed heterogeneous graphs face two structural challenges: symmetric graph aggregations cannot distinguish directional propagation mechanisms, and pure likelihood-based density scoring can underweight sparse localized perturbations. We address both within DA-GTNF, a unified framework for freeway anomaly detection. First, a direction-aware heterogeneous graph explicitly encodes four asymmetric relation types (downstream propagation, upstream shockwave, lateral lane interaction, and diagonal lane change), enabling a Relational GCN to learn independent aggregation kernels per relation. Second, a DualBranchGating mechanism fuses spatial graph embeddings and temporal Transformer embeddings through adaptive, feature-wise gates derived from unfused outputs. Third, a Dual-Conditioned Normalizing Flow estimates exact token-wise conditional log-likelihoods of the gated embeddings, while an auxiliary reconstruction head improves sensitivity to micro-level deviations that may be weakly reflected by NLL alone. On FT-AED, DA-GTNF achieves an AUC of 0.7218, a Reporting Delay of −11.80 min, a Mean Localization Error of 2.23 miles, and 100% incident coverage. On PeMS with incomplete ground truth, the segment-level DA-GTNF variant also consistently improves Recall@K% over all compared baselines.
