Accurate and computationally efficient prediction of daily financial variables from accounting data is essential for practical industrial applications. In this study, we develop a predictive model based on an Echo State Network (ESN), a representative approach in reservoir computing, using real-world daily accounting datasets. For the task of predicting 31-day ahead cash balances and net working capital, the ESN outperforms the benchmark Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model by 1.324 percentage points in accuracy and surpasses the predictive performance reported in prior studies while achieving approximately 73 times faster training. Furthermore, we conduct a scenario analysis by feeding pseudo journal entries representing future scenarios into the model to examine changes in future financial variables. These results suggest that the ESN is a promising approach for structured accounting time series derived from double-entry bookkeeping, especially when the number of observations is limited, in terms of both predictive accuracy and training efficiency.
