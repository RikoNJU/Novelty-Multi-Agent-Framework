Early detection of heart disorders relies heavily on identifying abnormalities in electrocardiogram (ECG) signals, yet most deep learning methods depend on large annotated datasets, which are difficult to obtain in practice. We present a combined unsupervised framework that leverages the advantages of both Transformers and LSTMs for reconstruction-based anomaly detection. The model is trained only on normal ECG recordings, enabling it to learn the underlying rhythm of healthy cardiac activity. At inference, irregular beats stand out through higher reconstruction errors, separated by a simple threshold. On benchmark datasets, the framework shows strong generalization – achieving 96.67% accuracy on ECG5000 and 96.44% accuracy on MIT-BIH. In our design, we have a lead-specific strategy for PTB-XL where separate models are trained per lead to maintain the small morphological differences, rather than averaging signals. Overall, our proposed method provides accurate, interpretable, and scalable ECG anomaly detection alternatives that we hope will provide a valuable tool for automated monitoring and early screening of arrhythmias.
