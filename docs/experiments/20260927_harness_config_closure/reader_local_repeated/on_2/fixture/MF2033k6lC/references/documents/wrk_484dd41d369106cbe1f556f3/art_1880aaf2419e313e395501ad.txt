This study presents an extension of the conventional data warehouse query paradigm by introducing Multi-dimensional Graph eXpression (MGX), a multidimensional query framework designed to support Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) over graph-based business intelligence data. MGX extends the traditional Multi-Dimensional eXpression (MDX) syntax used in relational data warehouses by incorporating SQL-style analytical constructs, including GROUP BY, HAVING, and ORDER BY, thereby improving the expressive capability and flexibility of multidimensional analysis over graph warehouses. By bridging relational data warehousing technologies with graph-based analytical environments, the proposed framework addresses key limitations of traditional MDX in supporting graph-oriented analytics. It enables more effective exploration of semantically rich and socially connected knowledge embedded in networked datasets. Overall, MGX provides a practical methodological foundation for advancing graph warehousing and supporting next-generation network-centric business intelligence applications.
